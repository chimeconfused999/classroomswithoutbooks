# ClassroomsWithoutBooks — Project Context

## Origin

Vyom Kumar, a senior at Moreau Catholic High School, is running ClassroomsWithoutBooks as his Social Justice Capstone (instructor: Mr. Triplett, Spring 2026 course). The project is based on personal visits to rural villages in India, where classrooms share a small number of textbooks despite enrollment being near universal. Vyom tutors two children from his father's home village in math and English every Saturday over video call. Aayush is co-founder, focused on social media and outreach.
