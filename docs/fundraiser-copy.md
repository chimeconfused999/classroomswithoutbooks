# Fundraiser Copy — #ClassroomsWithoutBooks

One-line pitch, a short version (fundraiser page), and a longer version (story section). Reference only — the live GoFundMe description may differ slightly; check before editing.

## One-line pitch

Bringing books, supplies, and weekly tutoring to kids in rural India, one notebook, one Saturday call at a time.

## Short version (main fundraiser description)

I've visited rural villages in India and seen it firsthand: kids showing up to school every day, but sharing one notebook between three of them. Enrollment isn't the problem. Resources are.

This campaign funds books and supplies through two organizations doing this work well: Pratham and Room to Read. Every dollar goes straight to them. I never touch the funds.

It's not just a fundraiser. Every Saturday, I personally tutor two children from my dad's home village in math and English over video call. This is something I'm already doing, not a promise for later.

**Goal: $500**
- $5 = a full set of notebooks and pencils for one child, for a term
- $20 = grade-level readers for a classroom shelf
- $50 = a term of supplies for an entire small classroom

## Longer version (story section)

I went to India and visited some very rural villages, the kind you don't see on a tourist itinerary. What struck me wasn't poverty in the abstract. It was watching a classroom of forty kids share six textbooks, and having every adult around treat that as completely normal.

The numbers back up what I saw: according to the 2024 ASER report, only 23.4% of rural Grade 3 students in India can read at grade level, even though enrollment is above 96%. Kids are in school. They just don't have what they need once they're sitting in the room.

I couldn't fix a national education system. But I could do two things.

First, every Saturday, I tutor two children from my dad's home village over video call, math and English, one hour, no exceptions. It's small, but it's real and it's already happening.

Second, I started this campaign to get books and supplies to more classrooms like theirs, through Pratham and Room to Read, two organizations with verified, transparent track records in exactly this space.

100% of what's raised here goes directly to those organizations. I'm not a middleman for the money, just the person who decided this was worth doing something about.

If you read this far: even $5 does something concrete. Thank you.
